Original assets for OpenWorld / Port Meridian

All district geometry, characters, vehicle geometry, procedural animation,
world dressing and UI in Native/scripts were authored for this project.
Synthetic audio in Native/assets was created by generate_audio.py using
oscillators and pseudorandom noise. No third-party recordings, GTA/RDR
models, music, textures or animation libraries were downloaded or copied.
These original assets are covered by the repository's MIT license.

Godot 4.5.1 runtime and its bundled libraries/fonts retain their licenses;
see GODOT-LICENSE.txt and GODOT-THIRD-PARTY.txt. Official binaries/templates
are downloaded from godotengine/godot-builds and verified by SHA256 in CI.
